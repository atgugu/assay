## Adversarial debate

### Strongest case for GO

The wedge is currently **white-space**: §03_market.md #6 surveyed 14 named adjacent vendors (Placer.ai, Huq, Geoblink, Mytraffic, Telefónica LUCA, Vodafone Analytics, DT Motionlogic, Orange Smart Mobility, Carto, Mapbox, CrowdVision, WaitTime, INRIX, BeMobile, Movement Strategies, Vianova) and found **no public evidence** of any shipping a city-scale event-driven real-time crowd-density forecasting product to ≥3 paying EU cities (the FATAL-2 condition does NOT fire) [§03_market.md #6, §04_competition.md]. The Capability Profile in `CLAUDE.md` assumes an elite AI-augmented solo founder for whom the technical build (GNN + temporal-attention on telco aggregates + GTFS + weather + events) is non-binding; only data-supplier cost is open (Phase-A unresolved). The Live Nation Astroworld $280M litigation cost [12] is a real-money signal that the insurance carrier WTP `[E12]` is not fabricated — there is a known unmet underwriting need. The EU AI Act Annex III check came back conditional-not-prohibited [40]: the wedge probably escapes FATAL-3. The composite-platform framing (retail + RE + insurance + B2G) would push TAM above €1B and is technically a "growth narrative" the user has already named as their long-term direction (§01_clarification.md). And the macro tailwind is real — Eurostat tourism nights crossed 3B in 2024 [3], top-flight football revenue €26.8B in 2023 climbing toward €29B in 2024 [2], the demand-side is growing 10–14%/yr `[E14]`. Single ADVANTAGE tag in the running tally; the rubric does not require zero positives to rule NO_GO, but the GO case rests on the wedge being big enough to fund a €100M outcome — and it isn't, before any incumbent question.

### Strongest case against GO

The pre-registered FATAL-1 fires unambiguously: post-CoVe SAM is €33M point estimate (range €22–58M), and the threshold was €80M [§01b_falsifier.md #1, §03_market.md #1, #3, #10e Q5]. The headline #1 was originally €78M but the synthesizer's own CoVe re-sum in §10e Q5 dropped it to €33M when the optimistic stacking was unwound — this is the exact failure mode pre-registration was designed to catch. The SAM upper-band €58M is reachable only by including two `[ESTIMATE]` channels (`[E12]` insurance €15M, `[E13]` telco-resale €7.5M) without primary-source backing; strip them and SAM is €7–43M point €18M. Three of the seven pre-registered kill conditions either fired or partly fired: FATAL-1 (SAM), BLOCKER-4 (B2G procurement timeline — 12–24 months realistic, exceeding the 9-month falsifier threshold [§03_market.md #7]), and BLOCKER on the "why now" narrative (Itaewon EU regulatory pull not located in primary sources; Cities Mission €98M call has zero crowd-analytics line items [25][26]; telco APIs are 10+ years mature, not newly productized [27][28] — all three legs of the user's "why now" hypothesis collapse on primary-source verification [§03_market.md #5b, #10e Q3]). RISK-7 fires sharply: only 1 EU-relevant 2024–2026 funding round ≥€5M (Liftango, Australia-HQ) is identified — the VC-narrative leg requires ≥3 [§03_market.md #8]. The named EU comparables ceiling at €5–30M ARR after 6–8 years (Mytraffic, Vianova, Huq) confirms the SAM is not a measurement artifact. The Verdict Optimization Target gates all FAIL or UNCONFIRMED: speed-to-revenue FAILS on the B2G path (12–24 months versus the workspace's <3-month threshold) [§08_business.md, §03_market.md #7]; investor-attractiveness FAILS on present evidence (no comparable EU rounds at scale) [§03_market.md #8]; defensibility is UNCONFIRMED (data exclusivity hypothesized, never tested). Suppliers (LUCA, DT Motionlogic) own the data layer and customer relationships and could ship the wedge in weeks if the founder demonstrates traction — the moat is structurally weak.

### Why the verdict rules NO_GO

The mechanical rubric in `CLAUDE.md` is unambiguous: ≥1 FATAL not refuted in writing by a red-team → NO_GO. In Phase A the red-team (Step 11) is stubbed, so no written refutation is possible; the market-size-analyst's own CoVe self-check in §10e *confirmed* the FATAL by correcting €78M → €33M, which is the opposite of a refutation. The cheapest discriminating experiment (above) is precisely a primary-source test of `[E12]`; the disciplined response to the pro-GO case is **not** to manufacture a PIVOT to the insurance wedge inside this verdict (that would re-write the MVP scope without re-running Step 2 clarification or Step 5 market sizing under the new framing) — it is to rule NO_GO on the wedge as stated, and offer the insurance-data-feed wedge as a separate auditable seed via `/idea_verdict --pivot` if the founder validates the WTP first.

## Phase-A confidence caveat

This verdict was rendered against a Phase-A audit in which Steps 4 (triage), 6 (competition), 7 (customer), 8 (regulatory), 9 (technical), 10 (business), and 11 (red-team) were stubbed. The market-size-analyst's CoVe self-check in §03_market.md #10a–#10e substitutes partial red-team discipline and partial competition mapping, but does not substitute for: (a) 5+ named buyer interviews testing pain severity and WTP, (b) a full incumbent map including stealth/pre-product players visible only via job postings or M&A signals, (c) primary AI Act legal review under DG CONNECT 2024–2026 guidance, (d) priced quotes from DT Motionlogic / Telefónica LUCA / Vodafone Analytics / Orange Smart Mobility for one-city data scope, (e) GNN-vs-naive-baseline benchmark on a published crowd-flow dataset, (f) full unit-economics + CAC/LTV + capital-ask construction, and (g) a structured pre-mortem of 5+ failure trajectories. Because of these gaps, confidence is set to **medium** rather than high — high-confidence NO_GO would require the missing layers. Specifically: a Phase-B Step 7 customer-interview round could conceivably surface a pain pattern strong enough to invert the SAM-up-via-pricing path (i.e., €300k+ ACVs that push city SAM alone above threshold), and a Phase-B Step 9 technical step could conceivably surface a 10× cheaper data path (e.g., commercial mobility-data broker at €20k/city vs telco at €100k+/city) that improves margins enough to fund slower B2G procurement. Both are low-probability but non-zero, and the audit did not foreclose them. The cheapest discriminating experiment above (insurance-carrier WTP calls) addresses the largest single Phase-A gap (`[E12]` insurance WTP unsourced) at the lowest cost, which is the disciplined Phase-A→B handoff.
